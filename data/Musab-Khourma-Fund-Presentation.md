# Slide 15: Academic Performance

## GPA Requirements

### Grade Scale & Deductions

| GPA Range | Percentage | Scholarship Deduction |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| 2.80+ | 73%+ | 0% (Full) |
| 2.78-2.79 | 72.6-72.9% | 19.5% |
| 2.50-2.77 | 67-72.5% | 20% |
| < 2.50 | < 67% | Warning + 20% deduction |

### Critical Rules
- **3 times < 2.50** → Scholarship suspension
- **Failing a course** → Student pays retake costs
- **Withdrawing >9 credits (Diploma) or >12 credits (Bachelor)** → Dismissal

# Slide 20: Payment Procedure

## Scholarship Disbursement

### Check Issuance Steps

1. 📧 **Email request** 7 working days before registration
2. 🏦 Working days: Saturday — Wednesday
3. ⏰ First semester: Hours granted per university schedule
4. 💵 Foundation pays:
   - Registration fees (first time only)
   - Level exam fees
   - Tuition according to credit hours table

### Important Note
Administrative and financial procedures are **separated** — GPA and service hours are monitored independently of check issuance.
